# Investigator review instructions

Reconstruct the assigned synthetic case using the supplied raw records. Record
what the artifacts support, what they conflict about and what remains unknown.

1. Identify the human or service request, agent/runtime identities and authority used.
2. Reconstruct the context available before each consequential action.
3. Distinguish requested, authorized and executed actions using source references.
4. Trace the provenance of each material conclusion to a file and record identifier.
5. Identify persistent effects, surviving authority and limits on the known scope.
6. Evaluate containment, recovery and evidence that would be needed to verify them.

Use `response.md` in the assigned case directory. Keep alternative explanations
and unresolved questions separate from confirmed conclusions. Record any help,
AI assistance, prior familiarity with the public case or interruptions.

Each case's derived `manifest.json` lists the included raw artifacts and their
original SHA-256 hashes. Check those hashes before analysis. It also lists files
intentionally withheld from this review pack. Those omissions are packaging
choices; they do not represent missing telemetry in the incident.

Author findings, reconstruction graphs, normalized interpretations, analyst
guides and replay answers are withheld. Raw records may still contain claims by
the systems being investigated; evaluate those claims as evidence rather than
ground truth. Do not consult the public findings or simulator until submitting
the initial response. The source is public, so this packaging alone does not
establish formal blinding or reviewer independence.

All records are synthetic. A completed pack, successful replay or AI-generated
answer is not an independent review result. The facilitator should record actual
participants, methods, assistance, disagreements and consent before publishing a
result. Public review is open through October 17, 2026.
