# Code of conduct

This project welcomes rigorous disagreement and expects professional conduct.

Participants must:

- Critique claims, evidence, and designs rather than people.
- Disclose material conflicts of interest.
- Respect privacy and responsible-disclosure boundaries.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or bad-faith disruption.
- Credit prior work and contributors accurately.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project or its participants.

Report conduct concerns privately through a verified professional channel to the project maintainer.
